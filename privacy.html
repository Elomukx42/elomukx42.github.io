Privacy Policy

Applies to DEV'S FILM STUDIO Uploader. Last updated 6 October 2026.

What the app is

DEV'S FILM STUDIO Uploader is a private tool run by the owner of the DEV'S FILM STUDIO YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the studio's own videos to that channel, schedule their release, set thumbnails, add them to playlists and update their descriptions. It is not offered to the public and no other person signs in to it.

Data accessed

  • Through Google sign-in (OAuth), the app receives permission to manage the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • It reads the channel's own video and playlist details (titles, IDs, publishing status) only to manage those uploads.
  • It does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token and the upload schedule are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API. No data is sold or shared with third parties, and no cookies or analytics are used.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections (myaccount.google.com/connections). Deleting the local token file also ends the app's access.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact
